Withholding Tax (WHT)

A tax deducted at the source when specific payments are made, such as interest, rent, service fees, or dividends. The person making the payment (the "withholding agent") is responsible for deducting this tax and paying it directly to the Inland Revenue Department (IRD).

PAYE Tax (Pay As You Earn)

This is the system for paying income tax on employment earnings. Employers deduct this tax directly from an employee's salary or remuneration each pay period and remit it to the IRD on the employee's behalf.

Calculate

Income Tax

A tax imposed by the government on the total income generated by individuals and businesses within a year. This includes income from employment, business profits, investments, and other sources, after deducting permissible expenses and allowances.

Calculate

SSCL Tax (Social Security Contribution Levy)

A tax levied on importers, manufacturers, service providers, and wholesalers/retailers. It is calculated as a percentage (currently 2.5%) of the business's "liable turnover" and is collected to fund social security measures.

Leasing Calculations

This involves determining the fixed monthly payment (rental) for a lease. The calculation is based on the total value of the leased item (e.g., a vehicle), the interest rate, the duration of the lease (term), and any down payment or residual value.
